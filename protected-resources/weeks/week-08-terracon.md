# Future Ready™ · Week 8

## TERRACON — PATHWAYS, PEOPLE + WORK IN ACTION

### Career Exploration · Workplace Observation · Skills · Questions · Evidence

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**What can I learn about careers by seeing how real people, roles, skills and systems work together?**

---

## THIS WEEK

You are visiting Terracon to learn how work actually happens inside a professional organization.

Do not treat this as a tour.

Treat it as an investigation.

Your job is to notice:

- the different roles inside one organization
- how people enter those roles
- what education, credentials or experience may be required
- what technical skills matter
- what human skills matter
- how people communicate and collaborate
- how work moves from one person or team to another
- how technology supports the work
- what career possibilities you did not know existed
