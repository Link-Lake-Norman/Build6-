# Future Ready™ · Week 12

## MICHELLE BOUDIN — ON CAMERA

### Communication · Feedback · Adjustment · Composure · Confidence

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**What happens when I receive feedback, make an intentional adjustment and try again?**

---

## THIS EXPERIENCE

This week is not about being perfect on camera.

It is about discovering what happens when you:

**TRY → RECEIVE FEEDBACK → ADJUST → TRY AGAIN**

During the past several weeks, you have practiced:

- using real evidence
- telling professional stories
- answering unexpected questions
- listening before responding
- making a clear point
- supporting an idea
- disagreeing professionally
- responding under pressure
- recovering when you lose your place

Now you will see those skills in action.

Your first attempt is not the final product.

It is evidence.
