## THIS WEEK

You landed your first full-time job.

Your annual salary is:

# $45,000

That sounds like a lot of money.

But your salary is not the same as the amount of money you actually have available to spend.

Taxes, insurance, benefits, transportation, housing, food, savings, emergencies and everyday choices all affect what your paycheck can actually support.

This week is not about creating a perfect budget.

It is about experiencing the decisions.
