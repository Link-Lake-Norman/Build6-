# Future Ready™ · Week 5

## FROM POSSIBILITY TO OPPORTUNITY

### Career Exploration · Pathways · Relationships · Taking Initiative

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**How do I move from wondering what is possible to actually learning about and creating opportunities?**

---

## THIS WEEK

You do not need to know exactly what you want to do with your life.

Career exploration is not about choosing one permanent job.

It is about learning:

- what possibilities exist
- how industries actually work
- how many different roles can exist inside one organization or industry
- what education, training and experience can lead to those roles
- what skills people use
- how technology is changing work
- who you can learn from
- how relationships can create access
- how to take the next step professionally

By the end of this week, you should have **more possibilities, better questions and one real next move.**
