## BRAD McMILLAN — LIFE AFTER SPORTS + WHO REMAINS?

### Identity · Resilience · Reputation · Purpose · Transition

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**If athletics disappeared tomorrow, what parts of you would remain?**

---

# THIS WEEK

Athletics may be an important part of your life.

It may have shaped:

- your schedule
- your friendships
- your identity
- your confidence
- your discipline
- your opportunities
- your reputation
- the way other people describe you

But no role lasts forever.

At some point, every athlete experiences change.

That change may come through:

- graduation
- injury
- a roster decision
- a new level of competition
- changing priorities
- work
- family
- a new opportunity
- or simply the natural end of competitive sports

This week is not about walking away from athletics.

It is about making sure athletics is one part of your identity — not your entire identity.
