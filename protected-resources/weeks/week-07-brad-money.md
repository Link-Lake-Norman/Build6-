### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

---

## THIS WEEK

Last week, you experienced what a $45,000 salary can actually feel like when real-life expenses, tradeoffs and unexpected events are added.

This week, you will learn from a financial professional.

Your job is not to collect random advice.

Your job is to:

- bring real questions from your Week 6 experience
- listen for ideas that challenge your assumptions
- connect advice to your own decisions
- ask follow-up questions
- decide what applies to you now
- identify what still needs verification
- leave with one action you can use
