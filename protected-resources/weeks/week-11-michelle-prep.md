# Future Ready™ · Week 11

## MICHELLE BOUDIN PREP — FIND YOUR VOICE UNDER PRESSURE

### Pitch · Panel · Listening · Rebuttal · Professional Disagreement

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**How do I communicate what I believe clearly, listen to another perspective and respond professionally under pressure?**

---

## THIS WEEK

Last week, you practiced answering interview questions under pressure.

You learned that strong communication is not about memorizing perfect words.

This week, the challenge changes.

You will prepare for your experience with Michelle Boudin by practicing how to:

- make a clear point
- support it with evidence
- speak concisely
- listen before responding
- disagree without becoming defensive
- answer follow-up questions
- respond to another person's argument
- recover when challenged
- communicate confidently in front of a panel or camera

Next week, you will put these skills into practice on camera.

# PART 13 · RECOVER WITHOUT APOLOGIZING FOR EXISTING

**"Let me come back to the main point..."**

**"That's a good question. Let me think about that for a moment."**

If you need clarification:

**"Could you clarify what you mean by ________?"**
