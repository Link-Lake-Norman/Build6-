# Future Ready™ · Week 10

## INTERVIEW UNDER PRESSURE

### Confidence · Storytelling · Adaptability · Professional Communication

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**How do I stay credible, clear and confident when I do not know exactly what I will be asked?**

---

## THIS WEEK

Last week, you built a Story Bank.

You identified real experiences that prove teamwork, leadership, reliability, adaptability, problem solving and communication.

Now you have to use those stories under pressure.

An interview is not about memorizing perfect answers.

It is about:

- listening carefully
- understanding what the question is really asking
- choosing the right story
- explaining your actions clearly
- showing what you learned
- adapting when a question surprises you
- recovering if an answer starts badly
- communicating like a prepared professional
