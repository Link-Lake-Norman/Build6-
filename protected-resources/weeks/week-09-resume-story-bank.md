## RESUME STORY BANK + JENNIFER MILLER PREP

### Evidence · Resume Development · Personal Brand · Professional Communication

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**How do I prove what I can do instead of simply saying what I am?**

---

## THIS WEEK

You have already built evidence.

You have:

- explored careers
- made financial decisions
- asked professionals real questions
- observed workplace systems
- identified technical and human skills
- practiced professionalism
- reflected on what you learned

Now the challenge is turning those experiences into language employers can understand.

A strong resume does not simply say:

- hardworking
- leader
- responsible
- team player
- good communicator

It proves those qualities through evidence.

# PART 4 · ACTION + CONTEXT + RESULT

Use this structure:

**ACTION + CONTEXT + RESULT**

## EXAMPLE

Weak:

“Good team player.”

Stronger:

“Collaborated with teammates during a high-pressure season while balancing academics and travel commitments.”

Stronger with result:

“Collaborated with teammates during a high-pressure season, adjusted roles when needed and helped maintain preparation through changing schedules.”
