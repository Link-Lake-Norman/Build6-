# Future Ready™ · Week 14

## MENTORSHIP + INFORMATIONAL CONVERSATION

### Relationships · Curiosity · Listening · Follow-Up · Professional Initiative

### ESSENTIAL QUESTION

**How can I build relationships with people who can help me learn — without treating networking like asking for a job?**

---

# THIS WEEK

Last week, you identified people you may need on your Decision Team.

You considered people who could help you think about:

- career and work
- money
- education
- life and values
- athletics
- health and well-being

This week, you move from identifying people to actually learning how to build professional relationships.

A mentor is not someone whose job is to solve your future.

A strong professional relationship begins with:

**CURIOSITY → PREPARATION → LISTENING → FOLLOW-UP → ACTION**
