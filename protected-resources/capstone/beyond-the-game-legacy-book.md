# SIGNATURE COMPONENTS

## Wins & Losses Log

Keep a living record of meaningful wins and losses — on the field and
off.

Do not wait until the end of the year and try to remember everything.

Capture the moments while they are real.

---

## Voices That Shaped Me

Interview a coach, teammate or mentor who influenced your journey.

Their voice becomes part of your story.

---

## Game Film Reflection

If footage exists, revisit one meaningful game or athletic moment.

Ask:

**What do I notice now that I did not notice then?**

---

## Sealed Letter to My Future Self

Write to yourself as you are today.

Capture:

- what you hope for
- what you are worried about
- what you want to accomplish
- who you think you are becoming
- what you hope your future self remembers

This letter is a BEFORE snapshot.

It will be opened later so you can compare who you were with who you
became.

---

## Career-Field Interview

Speak directly with a working professional in a field you are exploring.

Learn about the real work, the pathway, the challenges and what they
wish they had known earlier.

---

## Underclassman Pairing

Later in the program, identify a younger athlete you can encourage or
mentor.

Your experience becomes something you can give to someone coming
behind you.

---

## Physical Legacy Book

Your finished Legacy Book is meant to become something you can keep,
reference and be proud of — not simply another assignment that
disappears after it is graded.

---

# WHAT COUNTS AS EVIDENCE?

Your Legacy Book can grow from:

- weekly Future Ready™ work
- reflections
- class activities
- workplace experiences
- speaker conversations
- professional feedback
- career exploration
- interviews
- challenges
- wins and losses
- projects
- Passport evidence
- internship experiences
- mentor relationships
- moments when your thinking changes

You do not have to know your whole future today.

You do need to notice your growth.

# WEEK 5 CONNECTION

## FROM POSSIBILITY TO OPPORTUNITY

This week's former-athlete conversation is part of your Legacy Book.

Listen for:

- what life looked like before athletics
- what athletics required
- what athletics taught
- what athletics did not prepare someone for
- how identity can become connected to a role
- what happens when that role changes
- transferable skills
- relationships and mentors
- career decisions
- what someone wishes they had built earlier

Ask yourself:

### What am I already building through athletics?

### What do I still need to build?

### Who am I becoming beyond the game?

### What should I begin now that my future self will be grateful I built?

Your Week 5 responses become evidence.

They are not just answers for this week.

They are part of your story.
